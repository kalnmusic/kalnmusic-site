About

KALN

Music that lives somewhere between feeling everything and saying just enough.

The sound changes depending on the story — R&B, pop, something softer, something darker, something that doesn’t need a label yet.

What stays the same is the intention behind it: honest writing, strong emotion, and songs that feel personal without needing to explain every detail.
