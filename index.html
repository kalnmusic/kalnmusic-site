Behind KALN

Music that sounds like the conversation after you finally say what you meant.

KALN is an independent Texas artist working in the space between R&B and pop.

The songs start with the writing: direct, emotional, a little restless, and more interested in saying something real than fitting into one exact sound.

From intimate records to bigger hooks, KALN is building the music one story at a time.
